Over the 9th and 10th of Muharram, Bahira came together in Mohalla Nawaban to set up a Sabeel for our community.

In the summer heat, we served around 30 litres of cold, sweet drinks to hundreds of passing travellers and locals. It was a humble effort to honour the spirit of this sacred time by spreading kindness and relief to those around us.

A special thanks to our five volunteers, who gave their time to prepare and serve the drinks: Faiz, Dawood, Ahmad, Abdullah and Hassan. Thank you for your hard work and big hearts.
