On the 21st of Ramzan, we served a warm iftar of rice, dates and drinks at Bahawalpur Railway Station.

It was a simple effort to share the blessings of this month with travellers and the people who work at the station. Seeing everyone sit down together to share the meal was the best part of the day.

A huge thank you to the volunteers who showed up and worked so hard to make it happen: Faiz, Daud, Dawood, Ahmad, Yasir and Abdul Rahim. We couldn’t have done it without you.
