To honour the memory of the children lost in the attack on the Army Public School in Peshawar on 16 December 2014, the Bahira team chose to spend the anniversary with the children at Daykh Bhaal Shelter Home.

We shared a meal of burgers and snacks, but more importantly, we shared smiles and hope.

We believe the best way to remember those we lost is to take care of those who are still with us. By looking after these bright young souls, we hope to turn a day of sadness into a day of kindness.
