Your donation pays for our events: the food, the drinks, and what it takes to bring them to the people who need them.

## Tell us about your donation {#report}

The account is in Bilal’s own name, so we ask everyone who gives to fill in this form. It lets us keep every donation apart from his own money, spend it on the cause you chose, and write back to confirm that it arrived. Keep the screenshot of your payment; if you like, email it to us at {email}.

## Zakat {#zakat}

Bahira accepts Zakat. If your donation is Zakat, tick “This is Zakat” in the form when you give, so that we keep it apart and spend it only on what Zakat may be spent on.

## Tax and registration {#tax}

Bahira is not registered yet, so donations to Bahira cannot be claimed against tax. We plan to register with the Punjab Charity Commission. Read more on our [accountability page](/accountability/).

## Giving from abroad {#abroad}

The account takes money from JazzCash, Easypaisa, NayaPay and SadaPay in Pakistan, or from a money transfer service abroad that sends to them. If you are abroad and need help with this, write to us at {email}.

## Be careful {#careful}

Bahira only uses the account on this page. If anyone asks you for money in Bahira’s name and gives you any other account, it is not from us. Please tell us at {email}.
