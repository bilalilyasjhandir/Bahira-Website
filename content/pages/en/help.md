Tell us what you need, and we will contact you on WhatsApp. Asking costs nothing, and you don’t need to give your CNIC number.

## What happens next {#next}

1. Bilal, our founder, reads your message.
2. We send you a message on WhatsApp at the number you gave, as soon as we can. If we need to talk, we call you after that.
3. If you would rather a woman contacted you, say so in your message, and a woman from our team will.
4. If we can help, we tell you how. If we can’t, we try to tell you who can.

## Who we can help {#who}

Bahira is a small group of volunteers in Bahawalpur. So far our help has been food and drinks at our events, and we cannot promise to help everyone. But we read every message, and we will be honest with you about what we can do.
