This website has no cookies, no tracking and no adverts. The only information we receive is what you choose to send us through a form.

## What our forms collect {#collect}

What you type in: your name, your phone number or email address, your city, what you would like to help with, and your message. If you tell us about a donation: the amount, the app you sent it to, its transaction ID, the cause you chose, and whether it is Zakat. We also note the language of the page you used, so we can reply in the same language.

We never ask for your CNIC number or your gender.

## Where it goes {#where}

When you press Send, your message goes to Web3Forms, a form service, which emails it to Bahira at {email}. Web3Forms says it does not keep the messages it passes on, and that it deletes its technical logs every two months.

## Who reads it {#who}

Bilal, our founder, reads every message. If you ask for a woman to contact you, he passes your message to a woman in our team. We use what you send only to reply to you, and we never sell it or pass it on to anyone else.

## How long we keep it {#keep}

We keep your message in our email for as long as we need it to help you or to work with you, and then we delete it.

## This website {#website}

The site uses no cookies, no analytics and no adverts, and it loads nothing from other companies: its fonts and photos come from the site itself. Like any website host, Vercel, which hosts this site, handles technical details such as your IP address in order to send you the pages.

## Your choices {#choices}

To see, change or delete anything you have sent us, write to {email}.
