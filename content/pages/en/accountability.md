# The money section is written for before donations open and before any arrive.
rewriteWhen: [donations-open, donations-received]
---

A charity should be open about who runs it and where its money comes from. This is where Bahira stands today. If anything is unclear, ask us at {email}.

## Registration {#registration}

Bahira is not registered yet. We plan to register with the Punjab Charity Commission, and we will say so here when we have.

## Who runs Bahira {#who}

Bahira was founded by {founder}, who runs it. There is no board and no paid staff yet. Volunteers join us for each event, through social media, family and friends.

## Money {#money}

So far, Bahira has not taken any donations. Bilal has paid for every event himself.

When donations open, they will go to a mobile account in Bilal’s name, which takes money from JazzCash, Easypaisa, NayaPay and SadaPay, until Bahira has an account of its own. The Donate page will say so plainly, next to the account. Everyone who gives will be asked to tell us about it in a short form, so that each donation is kept apart from Bilal’s own money and spent on the cause the donor chose. Zakat is kept apart from other donations, and spent only on what Zakat may be spent on. Donations will pay for our events: the food, the drinks, and what it takes to bring them to people.

## Photos {#photos}

We share photos to show what really happened. The people in them know they are being photographed for Bahira. We never share photos that show people as helpless.

Phone photos can record the exact place where they were taken. We remove that from every photo before it goes online. If you would like a photo of you taken down, write to us and we will remove it.

## Children and people at risk {#safeguarding}

When we work with children, we do it together with the people who look after them, such as the staff of a shelter home. Volunteers are never alone with a child.

We never publish the names or personal stories of the people we help, and we never ask anyone for their CNIC number to receive help.

## Complaints {#complaints}

If something we did worried you, tell us at {email}. Bilal reads every message and will reply. If it is about a volunteer, we will look into it and tell you what we did.

## Reports {#reports}

Once a year, starting after our first year, we will publish a short report: what we did, and what it cost.
