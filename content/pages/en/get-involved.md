Bahira runs on volunteers. Give a few hours to an event, bring a skill, or help more people hear about us. You don’t need any experience.

## What volunteers do {#roles}

At our events, volunteers get everything ready, carry and serve the food and drinks, and clear up afterwards. Every pair of hands counts.

We also need help with:

- taking photos and short videos at events;
- writing posts and sharing them;
- finding places and people who need help;
- teaching a skill to young people, once our youth work begins.

## How it works {#steps}

1. Fill in the form on this page. It takes two minutes.
2. We message you on WhatsApp or by email, to say hello and hear what you would like to do.
3. We tell you about our next event. Come when you can: there is no fee and no paperwork.

## Skills for young people {#youth}

Teaching modern skills to young people is one of the six parts of our work, and we have not started it yet. If you can teach something useful, such as computers, design, writing or a trade, or if your school or college would like to hold an event with us, tell us in the form.

## Spread the word {#share}

Not everyone can give their time. You can still help: follow Bahira, share our posts, and send the people you know a link to this website.
