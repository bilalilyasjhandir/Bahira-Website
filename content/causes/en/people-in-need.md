Food, water and care for people going through a hard time. This is where Bahira began, and so far it is all of our work.

## Why it matters here {#why}

In Bahawalpur there are people around us every day who could use a meal, a cold drink in the heat, or simply some company: travellers far from home, people working through the day, and children growing up without their parents.

## What we do {#what}

We take food and drinks to the places where people are: a shelter home, a railway platform, a street in Muharram. We serve them ourselves, and we stay to share the meal instead of handing it over and leaving.

## How you can help {#help}

Come and help at our next event: there is always something to carry, serve or clear up. If you know someone who needs help, show them our help form, or fill it in with them. Donations will open soon; with them, we want to hold an event at least once a month.
