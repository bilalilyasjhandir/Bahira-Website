03 — The logo
1234

One shape, four ideas

  1. The BBahira’s initial is the outline of the whole mark.
  2. The flame on topLight: the meaning of the name.
  3. The lamp (chiragh)Drawn in white at the base — the everyday oil lamp that keeps a home lit.
  4. The flame in the lampA second, smaller light carried inside the B.

The symbol and the name BAHIRA together are the logo. Always use them as supplied — never redraw, rearrange or recolour them.

04 — Redrawn in HD

The same logo, now sharp at any size

Your logo existed only as a 659 × 659 pixel picture, so it went blurry whenever it was enlarged. It has now been redrawn as vector artwork: every edge was measured on the original and rebuilt as clean lines and curves. Nothing was redesigned.

The lettering is not a typeface you can type — it was drawn with the logo — so BAHIRA was redrawn letter by letter too. The two A’s were very slightly different in the original (by a quarter of a pixel); they are now identical.

The name in Urdu

باہِرہ with a zer, so it is read Baa-hi-ra.
Lettering
Noto Nastaliq Urdu Bold, in the Nastaliq style Urdu readers know from books and newspapers.
Size
The Urdu name looks the same size as BAHIRA. The font is designed to sit beside capital letters of a set height, and that height is matched to BAHIRA’s letters.
Rules
The same colours, clear space and don’ts as the main logo. Like BAHIRA, the Urdu name is part of the artwork: always use the files, never retype it. Smallest sizes: Urdu 50 px or 15 mm wide; Urdu + English 80 px or 25 mm wide.

One blue, used with confidence

Bahira Blue is taken straight from the original logo. The other colours are built from it or from the warm off-white of the original artwork, so there is no second brand colour to compete with it.

Blue text on white has a contrast of 8.2 : 1 (on Paper 7.5 : 1), comfortably readable for everyone. CMYK values are a starting point for coated paper — always ask the printer for a proof. Check the Pantone match against a printed Pantone guide.

10 — Type
Aa
Onest

The letters in the logo were drawn by hand, so they are not a font. Onest is the closest free typeface to them — the same simple, geometric build, flat-topped A and straight-legged R — so text set in Onest sits naturally next to the logo.
